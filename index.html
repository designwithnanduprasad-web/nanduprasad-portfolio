You were going to ask

  1. 01

    Can you start soon?

    i’m open to new roles right now. send the brief and you’ll get questions back the same day.

  2. 02

    How long have you been at this?

    3+ years in design. 5 years before that leading a 25-person team, so deadlines don’t scare me.

  3. 03

    Isn’t it all just AI?

    AI gets me to a first draft fast. everything after that is photoshop, illustrator and taste.

  4. 04

    Who should not hire you?

    anyone who wants “like canva, but quicker”. i’ll push back when the brief is wrong. nicely. mostly.

  5. 05

    What gets my best work?

    a clear brief, honest feedback, and the logo file that isn’t a screenshot.

  6. 06

    What do you still suck at?

    leaving a layout alone. i will nudge it 2px at 1am.
