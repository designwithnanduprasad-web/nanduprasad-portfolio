Diya Al Shams

A food brand serving Dubai, Sharjah and Ajman. The letters D and S fold into one compact icon that sits on a cup lid as easily as on a shop sign.

Brand
Diya Al Shams
Sector
Food, UAE
Deliverables
Logo, concept, pattern, packaging, signage

01The mark

Built from the brand initials, then simplified until it worked as a single-colour stamp. Deep green and gold keep it warm without looking fast-food.

02Takeaway first

For a food brand, the cup and the bag travel further than the sign, so the pattern wraps the packaging and the icon sits where it is seen first.
