Steep

A launch campaign for a smart kettle, with every product shot and ad made with generative AI. No studio, no shoot.

Role
Concept, art direction, AI production
Scope
Key visual, lifestyle, feed, story and billboard ads
Year
2026
Tools
GPT Image 2.0 · Higgsfield · Photoshop

01The brief

Steep is a fictional smart kettle: schedule it from your phone and set the exact temperature for every tea. Launch budget: small. Studio shoot: not happening.

The self-set brief: a launch campaign that looks like a big-budget shoot, built entirely with generative AI and held to brand standard.

02The insight

Mornings are a race, and tea people are fussy. Green tea scalds at 100°, and nobody wants to stand and wait by the kettle.

Mornings, on time.

03How I made it

  1. Write the shot list first

    Every image started as a brief: product angle, light, props, and the one benefit it had to sell.

  2. Lock the product

    One product description reused in every prompt (sage body, steel spout, ring display), so the kettle stays the same kettle across formats.

  3. Two benefits, two lines

    Scheduling: “Boils before your alarm snoozes.” Precision: “Every tea, its temperature.” One umbrella: “Mornings, on time.”

  4. Design the system, then the ads

    Forest green, cream and sage, one condensed headline face, logo bottom-left. Feed, story and billboard all follow it.

  5. Check every frame

    Product details, type and logo checked frame by frame. Anything off-brand was regenerated.

04What I’d measure

I would A/B test the two benefit lines in paid social, compare cost per click against a stock-photo control, and log how many AI frames needed fixing, so the workflow is costed honestly.

This is a self-initiated concept project. Steep is a fictional brand I created to show how I think and work. The visuals were generated with GPT Image 2.0 (via Higgsfield) from my briefs and art direction.
