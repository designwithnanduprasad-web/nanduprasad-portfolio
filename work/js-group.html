JS Group

A Jamsheer Shafi Silver Hub, selling 925 Italian silver jewellery. The J and S run together in one fine line, like a piece of silver wire, so the mark feels made rather than typed.

01The mark

A single-line monogram that stays light at small sizes, so it can be stamped, foiled and engraved without losing detail.

The line “Silver whispers of luxury and prestige” sets the tone in English and Arabic.

02Packaging and store

Pink boxes and natural pouches keep the jewellery the hero. The shopfront uses the same mark in brushed metal.
