AI Ad Studio

Four video ads for a KILIG portable blender, each in a completely different style, made without a camera, a studio or a shoot day.

Product
KILIG portable blender
Role
Concept, prompts, AI production, edit
Format
Vertical social video, 10–15 s
Tools
Higgsfield · ChatGPT · Claude

01The idea

A brand needs different kinds of ads for different jobs: one to stop the scroll, one to explain the product, one that feels like a real person’s recommendation and one that shows the product in daily life. A shoot for each would take weeks. With AI, I can make all four from one product and test which one works.

02How I made them

  1. Script and shot list

    I wrote the concept, script and shot list for each ad with ChatGPT and Claude, then turned them into detailed image and video prompts.

  2. Keep the product right

    The blender has to look the same in every frame: the jar markings, the button labels and the KILIG logo. I generated with product references and rejected any shot where it drifted.

  3. Generate in Higgsfield

    Stills and motion were generated in Higgsfield, several versions per shot, and the best takes were picked by eye.

  4. Edit like a real ad

    Cut to the beat, keep it short (10 to 15 seconds), put the product on screen in the first second, and end on the logo.
