KILIG WC-18 Wine Cooler

A cinematic AI video ad and an eight-image Amazon listing for a compact wine cooler: small enough for a living room, quiet enough for a bedroom.

Client
KILIG · True North International
Product
WC-18 Wine Cooler, 18 L
Deliverables
AI video ad · Amazon listing
Ad made with
Higgsfield · ChatGPT · Claude

01The ad

The film sells a feeling before a spec. A cold mist rolls out of the cooler in a high-rise apartment, a man leaps across the room to catch a bottle, a drop of condensation runs down the textured side panel, and the last shot is the WC-18 floating in deep blue with grapes and ice.

Chilled to perfection, every time.

02The listing

Eight square images, each answering one buyer question in order: what it looks like, how big it is, how you set the temperature, how it’s built, why it’s quiet, why not just use the fridge, and what life with it feels like.

The listing uses deep navy and a cool blue glow throughout, the colours of cold, so the online images and the ad feel like one product.

03Design decisions

  1. Numbers you can picture

    “18 L” means little on its own, so the size image translates it into 4 bottles or 24 cans and puts the exact dimensions on the product.

  2. Show the hidden feature

    The thermostat knob sits on the back, so the image circles it, zooms in and says which setting to use: 1 for warmest, 7 for coolest, 4 for ideal.

  3. Beat the real competitor

    The real alternative is the kitchen fridge, so one image compares the two side by side: too cold and full of food smells versus a steady 4–16 °C, wine-only space.

  4. End with people

    The last image moves from product to a group of friends with the cooler beside the sofa, which is the reason someone buys it.

Real client project for KILIG (True North International). The video ad was made with AI (Higgsfield, ChatGPT and Claude), and the people in it are AI-generated.
