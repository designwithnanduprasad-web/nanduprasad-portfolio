Willow

A fashion label named after the willow flower. One letter carries both the name and the flower, framed in a diamond so it works as a badge on its own.

Brand
Willow
Sector
Fashion
Deliverables
Logo, symbol, pattern, signage, merchandise

01The mark

The W is drawn to echo the willow flower, a symbol of grace and femininity. The diamond frame around it turns the letter into a crest that still reads when the wordmark is dropped.

The same W repeats into a light pattern for fabric, packaging and prints.

02Out in the world

Gold on black for signage, gold on cream for packaging, and a blind emboss where the logo needs to whisper.

Design and mockups are my work.
